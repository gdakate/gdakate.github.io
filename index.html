About Me

I'm a first-year M.S. student in Data Science at Stony Brook University, researching at the CV Lab under Prof. Zhaozheng Yin. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, medical imaging, and real-world accessibility.

I care deeply about building AI that improves lives — helping clinicians segment polyps more accurately under low-annotation constraints, protecting user privacy through a 53%-faster face anonymization pipeline, and giving visually impaired shoppers real-time independence through a gesture-driven grocery app.

Beyond research, I bring a strong backend engineering background in Spring Boot, AWS, and GCP — giving me the full-stack perspective to take ML from prototype to production.

**Google ML Bootcamp / KSTA-NIPA**
**Mar 2025**

Improved a diffusion-based face anonymization pipeline while evaluating the privacy–utility tradeoff.

**Highlights**

- Reduced inference time by **53%**, from 18.6 s to 8.7 s per image.
- Used identity-exclusion logic and inference hyperparameter tuning.
- Built an evaluation pipeline using Re-ID, FID, and attribute-preservation metrics.
- Awarded **2nd Prize** at the 2025 KSTA/NIPA Google ML Bootcamp.

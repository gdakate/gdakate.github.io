**Team Lead / Backend & ML Developer**
**Mar 2024 – May 2024**

Developed an assistive real-time shopping system for visually impaired users using object detection, gesture recognition, audio feedback, and cloud inference.

**Highlights**

- Trained YOLOv8 on an expanded grocery dataset, reaching **83%** object-detection accuracy.
- Integrated MediaPipe gesture recognition for touchless control.
- Deployed a Flask inference service on AWS EC2 with sub-second latency.
- Work was published at the 2024 Korean Entertainment Media Society Fall Conference.

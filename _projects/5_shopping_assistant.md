**Mar 2024 – May 2024**

Assistive shopping application combining real-time object detection, gesture recognition, audio feedback, and cloud inference.

**Highlights**

- YOLOv8 object detection: **83%** accuracy on grocery dataset.
- MediaPipe gesture recognition for touchless interaction.
- Flask inference server on AWS EC2 with sub-second latency.
- Published at the 2024 Korean Entertainment Media Society Fall Conference.

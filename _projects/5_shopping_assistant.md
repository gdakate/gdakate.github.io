**Mar 2024 – May 2024**

**Team Leader, Backend and ML Developer**

Assistive shopping application that tells visually impaired users which product they are holding.

**Highlights**

- Fine-tuned a **YOLOv8** detector on grocery product images using transfer learning from a pretrained model.
- Combined it with **MediaPipe** hand tracking, using bounding-box overlap between the hand and detected products to identify the product in the user's hand.
- Built a **Flask inference backend deployed on GCP** that connected real-time recognition to a Flutter mobile app with audio feedback.
- **Gold Award**, Sookmyung IT Project Competition. Published at the 2024 Korean Entertainment Media Society Fall Conference.

**Stony Brook Medicine · May 2026 – Aug 2026**

Investigated how pretrained video foundation models represent and use spatiotemporal evidence in clinical video using frozen V-JEPA representations from 3,600+ ICU clips.

**Highlights**

- Built eye-, face-, and patient-level representation pipelines from 3,600+ ICU clips.
- Implemented Top-K Sparse Autoencoders with multi-seed stability tests, activation-based retrieval, optical-flow grounding, and artifact controls.
- Developed region–time counterfactual auditing for frozen MLP and self-attention predictors.
- Counterfactual evidence outperformed native attention by **+0.23 logit** while matching Integrated Gradients.

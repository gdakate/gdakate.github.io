**Research Project Assistant — Stony Brook Medicine**
**May 2026 – Present**

Studying how pretrained video foundation models represent and use spatiotemporal evidence in clinical video. Built an interpretability pipeline using frozen V-JEPA representations from 3,600+ ICU clips across eye-, face-, and patient-level streams.

**Highlights**

- Implemented Top-K Sparse Autoencoders in PyTorch and evaluated learned latent features through multi-seed stability testing, activation-based retrieval, optical-flow grounding, and artifact controls.
- Developed a region–time counterfactual auditing framework for frozen MLP and self-attention predictors.
- Counterfactual evidence outperformed native attention by **+0.23 logit** while matching Integrated Gradients.
- Research is ongoing.

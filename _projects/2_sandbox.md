**CSE 590 Research Project — Stony Brook University**
**Jan 2026 – May 2026**

Built a multiplayer social-deduction research environment in which humans and frontier LLM agents interact under shared anonymization, enabling controlled study of deception, behavioral signatures, and human–AI detection asymmetry.

**Highlights**

- Integrated Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama and other providers in a common multi-agent environment.
- Built an asynchronous FastAPI + WebSocket backend coordinating game phases and agents in real time.
- Designed structured JSONL logging for hints, chat, votes, reflections, and reasoning traces.
- A 5-session pilot found humans detected AI players at **73%**, compared with **40%** detection by AI raters.

**Jan 2026 – May 2026**

Multiplayer social-deduction research environment where humans and LLM agents interact under shared anonymization, designed to study deception and human–AI detection asymmetry.

**Highlights**

- Integrated Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Llama-family models in a unified multi-agent environment.
- Built FastAPI + WebSocket real-time multi-agent coordination backend.
- Structured JSONL logging for hints, votes, chat, and reasoning traces.
- 5-session pilot: humans detected AI players at **73%** vs. **40%** detection by AI raters.

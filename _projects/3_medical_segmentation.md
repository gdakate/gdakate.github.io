**Student Researcher — Stony Brook CV Lab**
**Feb 2026 – May 2026**

Investigated zero-shot and low-annotation medical image segmentation using vision foundation models on the Kvasir-SEG polyp segmentation dataset.

**Highlights**

- Reproduced EviPrompt and ProtoSAM-style zero-shot SAM experiments on Kvasir-SEG.
- Achieved DSC **34.85** compared with a reported **24.45** baseline.
- Controlled experiments showed support-image selection alone could shift mean Dice from **0.368** to **0.735**.
- Simple visual statistics failed to predict support quality, suggesting target–reference feature compatibility as the stronger factor.

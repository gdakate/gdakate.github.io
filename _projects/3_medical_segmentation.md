**Feb 2026 – May 2026**

Evaluated zero-shot and low-annotation medical image segmentation using SAM-based vision foundation models on the Kvasir-SEG polyp segmentation dataset.

**Highlights**

- Reproduced EviPrompt and ProtoSAM-style experiments on Kvasir-SEG.
- Achieved DSC **34.85** vs. reported **24.45** baseline.
- Support-image selection alone shifted mean Dice from **0.368** to **0.735**.
- Simple visual statistics insufficient for predicting support quality — feature compatibility matters more.
